🦥 Flashy Defense

A Toy Story-style night defense adventure

Chelsea, age 10, is fast asleep. Her sassy older sister, the Sassinator, has unleashed her evil twin stuffies Skyler & Honey and their minions to steal Chelsea's Orb of Power — actually a moon nightlight, but shhh.

Only Flashy, the Mighty Sloth, can save the night.

Controls

  • WASD — Move Flashy
  • Mouse — Look around · Left Click — Shoot foam darts
  • T — Toggle turret placement · click floor to place
  • 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 — Pick turret type
  • M — Mute / un-mute sound
  • Space — Hop (for style)

💨 Every now and then Chelsea farts in her sleep and a power-up appears near the bed. Grab it!
